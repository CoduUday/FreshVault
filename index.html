Crop-Specific Storage

Vegetable Storage Profiles

Different vegetables require distinct temperature and humidity ranges. The system uses crop-specific profiles rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

💡 Crop Preservation Principle: Storage conditions vary by crop. Storing chill-sensitive vegetables (such as tomatoes or cucumbers) at near-zero temperatures causes pitting and quality loss. The final system applies crop-specific setpoints.
🥔

Potato

Target Temp:8°C – 12°C
Target Humidity:85% – 90% RH
Holding Window:60–90 Days
Avoid chilling below 4°C to prevent cold-induced sweetening (sugar accumulation).
🍅

Tomato

Target Temp:10°C – 14°C
Target Humidity:85% – 90% RH
Holding Window:14–21 Days
Chilling sensitive below 10°C. Maintaining moderate chill avoids surface pitting.
🥬

Cabbage

Target Temp:0°C – 4°C
Target Humidity:90% – 95% RH
Holding Window:30–60 Days
Tolerates near-zero temperatures; requires high humidity to prevent leaf drying.
🥦

Cauliflower

Target Temp:0°C – 4°C
Target Humidity:90% – 95% RH
Holding Window:14–21 Days
High respiration rate. Needs quick post-harvest pre-cooling to prevent curd browning.
🥕

Carrot

Target Temp:0°C – 4°C
Target Humidity:95% – 98% RH
Holding Window:30–45 Days
Requires near-saturation humidity to prevent root desiccation and crispness loss.
🫘

Green Beans

Target Temp:5°C – 8°C
Target Humidity:90% – 95% RH
Holding Window:8–12 Days
Avoid storing below 5°C to eliminate russeting and surface pitting.
🥗

Leafy Vegetables

Target Temp:0°C – 2°C
Target Humidity:95% – 98% RH
Holding Window:5–8 Days
Extremely high transpiration rate; requires maximum humidity to avoid wilting.
🥒

Cucumber

Target Temp:10°C – 13°C
Target Humidity:90% – 95% RH
Holding Window:10–14 Days
Chilling vulnerable. Storing under 10°C causes rapid water-soaked lesions.
🫑

Capsicum

Target Temp:7°C – 10°C
Target Humidity:90% – 95% RH
Holding Window:14–18 Days
Maintain moderate humidity to prevent calyx mold while avoiding flaccidity.
🌶️

Chilli

Target Temp:8°C – 12°C
Target Humidity:85% – 90% RH
Holding Window:14–21 Days
High economic value in NER. Good storage longevity under controlled chill.
